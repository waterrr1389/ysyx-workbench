## Choice

16 B blocks, 4 sets, 2 ways, LRU (128 B): the lowest TMT among LRU caches that fit the area
budget (21626 um^2, 715 MHz). 256 B caches exceed 25000 um^2. Random replacement saves 6% of
TMT, under 1% of run time, and would make cachesim no longer an exact reference. No padding.
